How these numbers are calculated
  • Net cash flow is income minus expenses for the period. It is cash in and out of your accounts, not accounting profit.
  • Income is money that arrived and was not a transfer. Refunds count as income.
  • Expenses is money that left and was not a transfer.
  • Transfers between your own accounts, to savings, and (when the card itself is linked) credit card payments are listed separately and never added to income or expenses.
  • If a transfer is really income (a client paying by wire, say), or the reverse, change it with the Counts as menu on the Transactions page. Totals update straight away.
  • Comparisons use the same number of days. For a month still in progress, the percentages compare October 1 to today against September 1 to the same day, not against all of September.
  • Pending transactions are not counted until your bank posts them, because their amounts can still change.
  • Dates are the dates your bank reports. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

        How your information is protected

        • Your bank password stays with your bank.You sign in through Plaid’s secure window. Sumveri never sees your username or password.
        • Read-only access.Sumveri can view your transactions. It cannot move money or make payments.
        • Encrypted storage.Access keys and saved transactions are encrypted with AES-256 before they are written to disk.
        • You stay in control.Disconnect at any time to revoke access at Plaid and delete saved transactions.
